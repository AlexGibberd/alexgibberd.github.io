Overview

A stone terrace on the green edge of the city

Cromwell Road sits in a great spot on the edge of the city and is a really popular residential area with professionals, academics and families. It is a no-through road, so there is almost no passing traffic — quiet, low-traffic and safe, with a residents’ parking scheme that is a big plus locally.

Fairfield Nature Reserve is a few minutes’ walk from the door, and the nearby Lancaster Canal offers lovely countryside walks and an easy, safe commute to the university. The city centre is walkable too, with plenty of places to eat, shop and enjoy cultural attractions.

Behind the house, a back lane gives access to the rear gardens and keeps the bins out of sight. The garden itself is a beautiful, peaceful spot, framed by the magnificent gardens of the neighbouring houses.

Inside, the layout briefly comprises a hallway, lounge, dining room and kitchen, two cellar rooms, three bedrooms and a bathroom. Outside there’s a small front yard and a good-sized rear garden.

The house has undergone significant renovation, with a focus on practicality and energy efficiency. It is rated EPC B — unusually high for a stone terrace of this age, where the average across England and Wales is D.

Renovated with care
